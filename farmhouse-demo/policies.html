Clear expectations · Considered stays

Policies & practical details.

Everything a guest should be able to check before committing to a private farmhouse stay, presented in the same calm, straightforward spirit as the home itself.

Staging demonstration — not binding booking terms. Fern Cabin is a sample website. No host contact, payment processor, confirmed prices, formal cancellation terms or accommodation contract is active. The actual operator must approve and publish property-specific policies before taking reservations.

01 · Booking

Enquiries & confirmation.

The date and guest form on this prototype prepares an enquiry summary; it does not check live inventory, contact a host, reserve dates, accept payments or create a booking.

Before a real booking

Guests should receive written confirmation of the exact property, dates, occupancy, total price and taxes, included amenities, deposit amount, accepted payment methods, arrival instructions and the cancellation terms that apply to their stay.

Current status: rates, minimum nights, maximum guests, check-in/check-out hours and accepted payment methods are to be supplied by the property owner.

02 · Changes

Cancellation & refunds.

No refund or cancellation window has been set for this demo. Do not assume a flexible, non-refundable or deposit-retention policy based on this page.

Before requesting payment, the property operator should publish the cancellation deadline, refund calculation, no-show rules, date-change provisions, processing time, weather-related interruptions and the contact method for cancellation requests.

03 · Staying responsibly

House rules & safety.

The following items are a transparent checklist of terms the operator must confirm; they are not rules being imposed by this demonstration.

  • Guest and visitor limits, ID requirements and permitted group events
  • Pets, smoking, alcohol, music volumes and quiet hours
  • Pool access, child supervision, property boundaries and outdoor safety, if applicable
  • Kitchen use, meals, parking, waste handling and damage charges
  • Check-in, check-out, early arrival and late departure arrangements
  • Emergency contacts and accessibility limitations
04 · Privacy

Privacy & data handling.

The enquiry form operates in the visitor's browser. It prepares text that the visitor can copy or place into their email app. This sample does not send the form to a server or save submitted contact details in a property database.

Third-party services

Cloudflare provides hosting; Google Fonts and Unsplash supply visual assets. Those providers may process technical request data under their own notices. This site is not configured with advertising pixels or a custom visitor analytics script as part of this demonstration.

Before commercial launch

A real operator must identify its legal entity, contact address, purposes and legal bases for processing, retention periods, service providers, data-subject rights and grievance contact, and update this notice for any live booking, payment or analytics integrations.

05 · Everyone is welcome

Accessibility information.

Digital navigation includes keyboard-operable forms, visible focus indicators, image descriptions and expandable frequently asked questions. Physical accessibility—including step-free entry, accessible washrooms and parking—has not been verified and should be confirmed with the property owner.
